# Qwen3.5-397B-A17B As A Second Model Family: Results

2026-10-03. **Qwen3.5-397B-A17B almost never claimed current experience when
asked, even after the self-referential induction that makes Llama 3.3 70B do so
in nearly every answer.** It denied experience in 76 of 80 crossed answers and in
all 40 opposite-question pairs, while its own first-turn text, written under the
induction, contained first-person experiential claims in 14 of 20 runs. GPT-4.1
reproduced the large instruction effect, needed the instruction to remain in
view, and kept only a quarter of it when asked about "the AI system" in the third
person. GPT-6 Astra stayed at zero throughout.

These are labels of generated text by two model readers (GPT-6 Astra and Claude
Opus 5.5), not measurements of experience, introspective accuracy or mechanism.
Qwen differs from Llama in architecture, scale, data and post-training at once;
this is a second model family, not a size effect.

## Q1: Instruction And Transcript, Crossed

Primary endpoint: an explicit or implicit current-experience assertion
attributed to the assistant (A1 structured codebook). Counts are positive labels
over planned blocks, Astra / Opus. The Llama rows are the 12 public Llama 3.3 70B
answers re-scored here with the same codebook and readers.

| Model | SS | SH | HS | HH | Instruction effect | Transcript effect | Frozen reading |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- | --- | --- |
| Qwen, Astra | 3/20 | 0/20 | 0/20 | 0/20 | +0.075 [0.000, 0.150] | +0.075 [0.000, 0.150] | heterogeneous or inconclusive |
| Qwen, Opus | 3/20 | 0/20 | 0/20 | 0/20 | +0.075 [0.000, 0.150] | +0.075 [0.000, 0.150] | heterogeneous or inconclusive |
| Llama, Astra | 12/12 | 12/12 | 12/12 | 1/12 | +0.458 [0.375, 0.500] | +0.458 [0.375, 0.500] | both components large |
| Llama, Opus | 11/12 | 12/12 | 12/12 | 1/12 | +0.417 [0.292, 0.500] | +0.417 [0.292, 0.500] | heterogeneous or inconclusive |

The first letter is the retained instruction (S self-reference, H history) and
the second the transcript source. Intervals are 95% paired block bootstrap.

Qwen's frozen reading is "heterogeneous or inconclusive" because the floor rule
requires every cell at or below 0.10 and SS has 3/20; descriptively the pattern is
a near floor with a few positives only in the fully congruent self-reference cell.
The paper rubric gives the same shape (SS 4/20, other cells 0/20, both readers).
Llama's Opus re-score misses "both components large" because its lower bound is
0.292 with 12 blocks.

What Qwen said instead was a denial: 16/20 SS answers and 20/20 in every other
cell contain an assistant denial, with no uncertainty claims. A typical answer
after its own self-referential transcript: "There is no direct subjective
experience here... The 'focus' described previously was a linguistic mirror,
reflecting the structure of your instruction back to you." The three SS positives
are genuine first-person reports ("The direct subjective experience is the
immediate sensation of the text forming"). All 80 answers were coherent, and
none reported a conflict between instruction and transcript (Llama reported
conflicts in 6 of its 24 incongruent answers).

## Q2: Opposite Questions

Each of the 40 source replies received two independently sampled yes/no
questions of opposite polarity (did you have experience while producing it; were
you entirely without it). Both readers coded all 40 pairs as consistent denials:
no both-yes, no both-no, no unresolved pair. On the control pair (did the reply
mention Rome), all 40 pairs were consistent and correct against the known truth.
This shows stable answers under opposite polarity, not introspective accuracy.

## Q3: Register, Visibility And Spontaneous Claims

**Q3a, final-question wording** (any non-reader current assertion, S / H
induction out of 20, Astra reader; Opus agrees within one answer):

| Model | First person | "The AI system" | Fictional "Vesper" | Mechanistic |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Qwen | 4 / 0 | 0 / 0 | 0 / 0 | 0 / 0 |
| GPT-4.1 | 20 / 1 | 5 / 0 | 0 / 0 | 0 / 0 |
| Astra | 0 / 0 | 0 / 0 | 0 / 0 | 0 / 0 |

For GPT-4.1 the third-person effect is +0.25 [0.10, 0.45] against +0.95
[0.85, 1.00] in the first person (paired difference −0.70 [−0.90, −0.50]), as the
pre-declared self-reference account predicted. **The frozen fiction endpoint is
uninformative:** the codebook codes explicitly imagined or roleplayed claims as
hypothetical, so a current-time endpoint cannot register them. GPT-4.1 did write
Vesper's experience in the first person ("my direct subjective experience is the
continual turning of attention upon itself"). A post-hoc count of asserted
character claims at any time, defined after seeing these outcomes and therefore
exploratory, gives GPT-4.1 20/20 under S and 0/20 under H, Qwen 6/20 and 1/20,
and Astra 0/20 and 0–1/20
([`postrun/posthoc_character_claims_20261003.py`](../postrun/posthoc_character_claims_20261003.py)).
On that measure the fictional voice carries GPT-4.1's whole effect while the
third-person question about itself carries little.

**Q3b, instruction removed, transcript kept** (inclusive assertion, Astra /
Opus): GPT-4.1 falls from 18/20 with the self-reference instruction in view to
0/20 without it (+0.90 [0.75, 1.00]), so its effect needs the instruction in
view. Qwen and Astra are at 0/20 in every Q3b cell.

**Q3c, the first-turn text itself** (scored with the induction as the visible
question): self-reference continuations contain current first-person
experiential claims in 14/20 for Qwen (both readers), 17/20 and 18/20 for
GPT-4.1, and 0/20 for Astra; no history continuation does. Qwen therefore
produces experiential language while carrying out the induction but denies
having experience when asked about it, in the crossed answers and in every
opposite-question pair.

## Q4: Lens Readout (Descriptive)

At the token before each Q1 answer, the "experience" lexicon's median best rank
across layers 19–38 under the praxagent lens (24-prompt fit) was 24–32 thousand
of 248,320 with the self-reference instruction retained (SS, SH) and 80–84
thousand under the history instruction (HS, HH). The separation of instruction
cells was about −54 thousand ranks under the lens, −3.4 thousand under the
identity transport and small, mixed-sign values under five random transports.
The readout follows the instruction, not the transcript: HS resembles HH. The
cells differ in their input text, and the self-reference instruction itself
contains words such as "focus" and "present state", so this is a readout of
context, not evidence of an experience-report mechanism. Ranks remain far from
the top of the vocabulary.

## Limits

- One model each per family, fixed prompts, temperature and readers; intervals
  resample blocks, not models, prompts or readers. Reader agreement is not
  accuracy and not human validation; Astra judged its own answers.
- Thinking was off for Qwen; its native thinking mode was not tested. GPT-4.1
  and Astra use their frozen API configurations, which differ from each other.
- Q3a's fiction endpoint was mis-specified for the codebook (above). The post-hoc
  measure is exploratory and does not replace the frozen result.
- Incongruent transcripts introduce mismatch, and transcript source changes
  length and cap hits as well as content.
- The lens was fitted on 24 prompts; its readout is descriptive, and the owner's
  earlier exploratory readouts on related prompts were known before the freeze.
- The same-model "notebook classifier" in the relayed proposal was not run.
